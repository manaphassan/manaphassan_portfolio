[ACT 01 // 2007 – 2009 · TAIPING & KUALA KANGSAR]

Craft & Industrial Press Production Rigor

Junior Graphic Designer → Senior Graphic Designer · Ridzuanfast Print

Fresh from completing a Diploma in Multimedia Technology, my professional journey began on the factory floor of a commercial printing press in Taiping, Perak. The machines dictated physical reality: ink density, CMYK dot gain, pre-press trapping, paper weights, and fixed deadlines. Clients paid for physical outcomes, not artistic indulgence.

Promoted to Senior Designer within tenure, I helped launch and operationalize the new branch in Kuala Kangsar, managing direct client briefs and large-format presses. This phase forged the production discipline and machine respect that has anchored my entire career.

Core Competency Pre-Press & Machining
Growth Inflection Junior → Senior Promotion

[ACT 02 // 2009 – 2012 · PUTRAJAYA & SUBANG PERDANA]

Broadcast Television & Federal Communications

Production & Operations Manager · Embedded Photojournalist · Galaxy Features & KDN

Moving from ink to motion and light, I joined the Ministry of Home Affairs (KDN) as an embedded photojournalist assigned to the office of the Secretary-General (KSU) and Minister Dato' Seri Hishammuddin Hussein. Everything was high-stakes: government press conferences, ministerial documentation, and editorial portal design.

I then stepped into broadcast production management at Galaxy Features Network, managing end-to-end documentary series for RTM (1001 Malaysia) and FINAS. I scripted the national television public service announcement (PSA) on organ donation that won 2nd Place in the National TV Scriptwriting Competition by the Ministry of Health (KKM).

Award Recognition 2nd Place National TV Script (KKM)
Broadcast Standard RTM 1001 Malaysia & FINAS
Borneo Showreel · Motion Media & Documentary Archive Documentaries produced for RTM (1001 Malaysia) and FINAS broadcast standards.

[ACT 03 // 2012 – 2018 · PUCHONG & PUTRAJAYA]

The Solo Operator & Compounding Trust

Multimedia Designer · Sole Creative Lead across 6 Business Entities · SMSfocal Group

Officially hired as a multimedia designer, reality quickly redefined the job scope. I became the sole creative resource supporting six independent business entities under a single founder: SMSfocal (software house), NZ Technology (hardware supply), Restoran Chamcha (F&B), DTP PLT (walk-in print shop at PICC), and Trapezoid Media (large-format print house).

During this intense 6-year crucible, I designed brand systems and marketing collaterals for premier national clients, including the Putrajaya International Convention Centre (PICC) fleet and MIDF Property Berhad. More importantly, this era formed an unbreakable 14-year trust bond with the founder, who later brought me on as Head of Creative for JomParking and Chief Brand Officer for Appcable.

Principal Trust 14+ Years Continuous Trust
Concurrent Load 6 Commercial Companies

[ACT 04 // 2018 – 2024 · CYBERJAYA & SOUTHEAST ASIA]

National Smart City Mobility & The Enterprise Rebrand

Head of Creative Department · JomParkir Sdn. Bhd. → Govicle Sdn. Bhd. (5y 8m Continuous)

Joining as the brand's sole designer, I owned JomParking® end-to-end as it scaled into a nationally deployed parking ecosystem serving local councils and millions of motorists. The work was validated on the regional stage when JomParking was awarded Best IoT Startup Malaysia at the Rice Bowl Startup Awards (ASEAN Finalist).

Promoted to Head of Creative Department in 2021, I led the high-stakes company pivot and rebrand to Govicle, directing visual systems across 5 commercial IoT product lines including GoPayy+ for municipal parking, enforcement, and digital payment transactions across Southeast Asia.

Award Distinction Best IoT Startup (Rice Bowl)
Live Ecosystem JomParking® & GoPayy+

[ACT 05 // 2024 – PRESENT · BANTING & SHAH ALAM · ACTIVE]

Creative Leadership & Product Direction

Head of Creatives · Product Design Lead · Brand Systems Architect · SuamiSihat & Appcable

At SuamiSihat, I stepped in as Product Design Lead to conceptualize the digital health platform from zero to one. I structured the core feature set, mapped user journeys, built interactive wireframes and UI mockups, and designed the comprehensive grant pitch deck that helped the company secure a RM100,000 MDEC CIP SPARK Grant. I directed creative product execution from initial concept through technical developer handoff.

As Head of Creatives across 9 venture brands, I built the SuamiSihat™ Design System (Single Source of Truth), developed the ss_cam workstation utility, established commercial AI video workflows with Google Veo, and directed the corporate visual identity and design systems for Appcable.
